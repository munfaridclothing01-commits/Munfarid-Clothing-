About

Built for those who stand apart.

THE IDEA

Not for everyone.

Munfarid Clothing is a premium streetwear label built around a simple idea: you don't need to follow the crowd to make an impact.

Our aesthetic is intentionally monochrome, clean and confident — designed for people who want their clothing to feel different without trying too hard.

From heavyweight essentials to statement-inspired pieces, every Munfarid product is created to bring attitude, comfort and a distinct identity to everyday fits.
